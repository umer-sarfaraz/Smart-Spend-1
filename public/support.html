Common questions

How do I cancel my subscription?

Apple manages subscriptions. On your iPhone, open Settings, tap your name, then Subscriptions, and choose Penny Roost.

I paid, but the app does not show my plan

In Penny Roost, open Settings › Account › Subscription and tap Restore purchase.

Moving to a new phone

On the old phone, open Settings › Data & privacy › Export data and save a Backup file. On the new phone, open Settings › Data & privacy › Restore and choose that file.

Deleting your account

Settings › Account › Delete account removes your account and everything synced to it. Expenses kept only on your phone are not affected.
